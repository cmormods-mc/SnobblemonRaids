## General rolls

Category weights total 100 per tier and apply to every general selection, standard or contribution-earned.

| Category | starter | powerhouse | legendary | mythical |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| base/<tier> | 74% | 70% | 66% | 62% |
| general/leaf/cards | 12% | 14% | 16% | 18% |
| general/leaf/curios/<tier> | 14% | 16% | 18% | 20% |

A category at weight *w* appears at least once in a bundle with probability `1 - (1 - w/100)^2`. For the 21% starter card category that is 37.59%.

Raid keys and key fragments are not in the loot at the moment.
