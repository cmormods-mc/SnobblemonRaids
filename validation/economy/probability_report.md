# Reward-economy probability report

Generated by `validation/economy/build_tables.py`. Every figure is exact -- computed from the table weights, not sampled.

Each claim is **2 general selections + 1 specialty selection**, plus B further general selections earned by contribution (B = 0..3), so 3 to 6 in total. The specialty selection happens exactly once whatever B is, which is what keeps premium drop rates independent of how hard a player fought.
